# Enterprise Tier

**Classification:** Commercial / MDM / Sideload  
**Access:** Standard Android, sideload + MDM, no consumer channel  

---

## RealWear
- Standard Android enterprise glasses
- No consumer channel
- Sideload + MDM supported

## DigiLens ARGO
- Runs **DigiOS** on AOSP 12
- DigiLens also sells waveguides standalone
- No consumer channel

## Iristick
- SDK gated behind **paid NDA program**
- ~€1,137.50 first year, then €1,000/yr
- **Not recommended** — worth knowing about, not worth pursuing

## ThirdEye
- Standard Android enterprise glasses
- No consumer channel
- Sideload + MDM supported

## Summary
These are all viable if you have an enterprise use case and budget, but none offer the hardware openness or developer freedom of the top-tier platforms. DigiLens is notable for selling waveguides separately.

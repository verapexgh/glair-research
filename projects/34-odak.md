## What It Is
An **open-source computational-display and holography toolkit** covering:
- **Computer-generated holography (CGH)**
- **Perceptual graphics**
- **Lensless cameras**

## Why It Matters
Not a product. The right **starting point** if near-eye display design ever becomes in-house work rather than "buy a module from Raontech/Lingxi/Vuzix."

If your project ever needs to:
- Design custom holographic waveguides
- Optimize display optics computationally
- Build lensless camera systems
- Understand perceptual rendering for near-eye displays

...Odak is the open toolkit to begin with.

## Relevance to GLAIR
The "display is a part number" conclusion from Pass 2 means most projects will buy pre-aligned modules. But if you ever need to:
- Push beyond available module specs
- Design for a form factor no commercial module fits
- Differentiate on optical performance
- Understand the physics your supplier is implementing

...Odak provides the computational foundation.

## Comparison
| Tool | Focus | Stage |
|------|-------|-------|
| **Odak** | Computational holography, perceptual graphics, lensless | Research toolkit |
| **Brilliant Labs Frame RTL** | FPGA + MCU + micro-OLED | Working product, full source |
| **Raontech/Lingxi modules** | Pre-aligned LCoS/waveguide | Commercial module |

## Recommendation
Bookmark for future reference. Not an immediate action unless you're already planning custom optical design. But if the project ever graduates from "buy a module" to "design the optical path," this is where to start.

# Dead Ends

**Purpose:** So nobody re-researches them.

---

## North / Focals
- Acquired by Google in 2020
- Focals 2.0 cancelled
- IP absorbed
- North had also bought **Intel's Vaunt** patent portfolio — both now inside Google
- **No licensing path**

## Google Glass Enterprise 2
- Sales stopped March 2023
- Support ended September 2023
- **Project Iris** shelved 2023

## ODG (Osterhout Design Group)
- Collapsed
- Patents dispersed
- **[UNVERIFIED — needs checking if the IP matters]**

## Snap Spectacles Developer Program
- Published subscription developer program was withdrawn without notice
- $2,195 Specs pre-order is now the only listed route to hardware
- See full profile: `09-meta-androidxr-snap.md`

## Summary
These represent significant sunk investments by major companies. The lessons from their failures (battery, social acceptance, display technology readiness, enterprise vs. consumer fit) are as valuable as the hardware that succeeded. North's acquisition by Google and subsequent absorption of Intel's Vaunt portfolio means those innovations may resurface in future Google products, but with no external access.

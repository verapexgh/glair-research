# HumanHUD

**Classification:** New Find — Smart Glasses HUD App  
**Website:** https://humanhud.ai  
**Discovered:** 2026-09-03 via Hacker News  

---

## What It Is
A **web app/HUD service** that displays personal life statistics at the edge of your vision on smart glasses. Think of it as a "personal dashboard for your face."

## Supported Platforms
| Platform | Status |
|----------|--------|
| **Meta Ray-Ban Display** | **Available now** |
| HumanHUD.ai (browser) | **Available now** |
| iOS App | Coming soon |
| **Android XR Glasses** | Coming soon |
| **Snap Spectacles** | Coming soon (developer preview) |

## Features
- **8 live widgets:** Heart rate, steps, calories, portfolio, net worth, spending, weight
- **Health integration:** BPM, steps, sleep, calories
- **Wealth integration:** Portfolio, stocks, crypto (via Plaid bank sync on paid plans)
- **600² pixel canvas**
- **Always-on, 24/7**
- Receipt & nutrition label scanning

## Pricing
| Tier | Price | Features |

## RayNeo iO AI Glasses

### Status
**Coming soon.** "AI Smart Glasses with Invisible Display"

### Key Differentiator
- **Heads Up Display with no camera**
- Similar positioning to Mentra Mach1 and Vuzix Z100 — display-only, camera-free
- Privacy-forward by design (no camera avoids the Meta glasses backlash)

### Significance
RayNeo is expanding beyond their tethered USB-C display glasses (Air series) into an **AI glasses with integrated display** but without camera — a deliberate architecture choice that sidesteps the privacy controversies plaguing Meta.

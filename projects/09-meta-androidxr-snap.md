# Meta, Android XR, and Snap

**Classification:** Best Hardware, Least Control  
**Note:** Included for completeness, not because they fit the openness requirements.

---

## Meta Wearables Device Access Toolkit (DAT)
- Free public SDK
- Camera streaming + photo + mic/speaker
- Since ~May 2026: **display components** (text, images, lists, buttons, video) on Ray-Ban Display
- HTML/CSS/JS "Web Apps" path
- **Everything routes through the Meta AI app**
- Every tester must be invited by email
- **Public publishing is partner-gated**
- Meta AI itself is closed
- Developer Mode runs your build on your own glasses only

## Android XR
- Jetpack XR SDK is public
- Developer Preview 3 available
- **Samsung Galaxy Glasses** unveiled 2026-07-22
  - Snapdragon AR1 Gen 1
  - Camera
  - Open-ear audio
  - ~9 h battery
  - Privacy toggle
  - Audio-only — display version expected 2027
- **XREAL Project Aura**
  - Wired
  - 70° FOV optical see-through
  - Launching before end of 2026
  - ≤$1,500
  - Dev kits ship through the **Android XR Developer Catalyst Program** (apply at g.co/dev/catalyst)
  - First cohort strictly limited

## Snap Spectacles
- Published subscription developer program was **withdrawn**
- $2,195 Specs pre-order is now the only listed route to hardware

## Summary
If you need absolute cutting-edge consumer hardware and are willing to trade control for it, these are the options. For programmatic control and independence, look elsewhere.

- Contact: **vanda@topaiglasses.com**

### Antawei / 安塔威
- **Mid-size ODM with unusually deep vertical integration:**
  - Own **wearable OLED display-module plant in Dongguan**
  - Own **finished-device assembly plant in Huizhou**
  - Equity stake in **injection-moulding plant**
- **Shipping now:** ¥399 (Broadcom silicon) and ¥599 AI glasses
- **Planned:** 13 MP livestreaming camera glasses at **~¥999** on Anyka silicon
- **Executive contact:** **鄢鹏飞 (Yan Pengfei)** — quoted in trade press
- **Profile:** Would actually take a small first order. The partner profile for a US brand with a defined application.

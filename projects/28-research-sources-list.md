# Research Sources — Smart Glasses / AR / Wearable Computing

**Date:** 2026-09-03  
**Purpose:** Comprehensive list of internet sources for finding subject matter on smart glasses, AR hardware, embedded systems, supply chain, and related technology. Hand this off to a researcher who can browse the web.

---

## 7. Supply Chain & Sourcing (The Deep Layer)

| Platform | What It Is | How to Use It |
|----------|-----------|---------------|
| **Alibaba** | B2B marketplace | Search "AR glasses module," "micro OLED display," "LCoS optical engine" — but it's the English storefront, prices are inflated |
| **1688.com** | **The real** Alibaba — Chinese B2B wholesale | Cheaper prices than Alibaba, but Chinese interface. Use with translation. This is where ODMs actually buy. |
| **Taobao** | Chinese C2C/B2C — consumer facing | Buy OSAIG kits, dev boards, odd components. Needs forwarder for US. |
| **LCSC** | `lcsc.com` — electronic components | The BOM source for most open hardware projects. Prices, stock, datasheets. |
| **JLCPCB** | `jlcpcb.com` — PCB fab + assembly | The manufacturer behind most open hardware. Can trace who else is ordering similar boards. |
| **Seeed Studio** | `seeedstudio.com` | Open hardware components, Grove ecosystem, dev boards |
| **DFRobot** | `dfrobot.com` | Similar to Seeed, more maker-focused |
| **DisplayModule** | `displaymodule.com` | The catalogue with the Sony ECX343ENA module (DMGTX0068WUNA) |
| **Mouser / Digi-Key** | `mouser.com` · `digikey.com` | US/EU component distributors — check availability, get datasheets |
| **Findchips** | `findchips.com` | Cross-distributor search for chip availability and pricing |

## 12. Suggested Priority Order for Daily/Weekly Checks

If you're a researcher with web access, check these in this order:

### Daily
1. **Hacker News** — `news.ycombinator.com` · API: `hn.algolia.com/api/v1/search_by_date?query=smart+glasses&tags=story`
2. **r/smartglasses** — Reddit community pulse

### Weekly
3. **TheElec (Korea)** — `theelec.net` — display/OLED/microLED news
4. **Digitimes** — `digitimes.com` — supply chain leaks, BOM rumors
5. **集微网 / 芯智讯** — `jiwei.com` / `iczoom.com` — Chinese semiconductor, 1–2 weeks ahead
6. **OSAIG / Brilliant / Mentra Discords** — real current-status discussions

### Bi-Weekly / As Needed
7. **FCC filings** — `fccid.io` — search "smart glasses," "eyewear computer" for unannounced products
8. **YouTube teardowns** — Fomalhaut for new Meta/Samsung/Apple hardware
9. **LinkedIn** — follow key people at Raontech, Lingxi, SmartXY for job-posting hints
10. **Crunchbase** — track new smart glasses startups and funding rounds
11. **arXiv weekly** — `export.arxiv.org/api` — `cs.CV`, `eess.SP`, `cs.RO` for edge AI, computer vision

### Quarterly / Deep Dives
12. **SEC EDGAR** — Vuzix filings for financial health
13. **Patent searches** — USPTO/EPO for waveguide, LCoS, microLED, optical combiner IP
14. **1688.com** — real wholesale pricing on components
15. **Wayback Machine** — check dead product pages, pulled press releases

## Notes for the Researcher

- **Chinese sources are essential.** Most of the smart glasses supply chain is in Shenzhen/Dongguan/Seongnam. English coverage is 3–6 months behind Chinese trade press.
- **TheElec and Digitimes are the primary sources** that English outlets re-report. Read them directly.
- **FCC filings settle ODM questions.** The "grantee" name on an FCC filing is the actual legal manufacturer. If Mentra Live's grantee is SmartXY, that's confirmation.
- **Discords have the honest answers.** Official docs say one thing. Discord users say what actually works.
- **1688.com > Alibaba for pricing.** Alibaba is the English markup layer. 1688 is where ODMs actually buy.
